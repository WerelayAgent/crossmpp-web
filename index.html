x402 and MPP solved one-time.
CrossMPP solves recurring.

x402 and MPP define how an agent pays for a single request. Neither says anything about what happens next: renewals, billing cycles, budgets. CrossMPP adds that layer, with on-chain subscriptions, allowances, and metered billing for AI agents, settled on pump.fun in under a second for a fraction of a cent.
